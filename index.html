Privacy Policy

Brainrot TCG · Last updated: August 20, 2026

1. Overview

Brainrot TCG is a free, offline card game developed by Tomasz Dziedzic and Leon Dziedzic. Your privacy is important to us, and this policy explains how we handle your information.

2. Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal data.

  • No account or registration is required.
  • No personal information (name, email, phone number) is collected.
  • No usage analytics or tracking tools are used.
  • No cookies or similar technologies are used.

3. Data Storage

All game data (settings, language preference, deck size) is stored locally on your device only. We have no access to this data.

4. Third-Party Services

The app does not use any third-party services that collect data, including but not limited to: advertising networks, analytics services, or social media integrations.

5. Internet Access

The game does not require an internet connection and does not transmit any data over the network.

6. Children's Privacy

Since we do not collect any data from anyone, no special provisions for children are necessary. The game is suitable for all ages.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at:
